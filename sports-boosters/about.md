### Our mission

> The Cameron Sports Boosters Association exists to support, promote, and advance youth athletic opportunities within the Cameron R-I School District.
{: .mission-statement}

The sole mission of the Cameron Sports Boosters Association is to enhance and progress youth sporting activities by providing financial support, volunteer assistance, fundraising efforts, recognition programs, equipment, facilities, and other resources that benefit student-athletes and athletic programs.
